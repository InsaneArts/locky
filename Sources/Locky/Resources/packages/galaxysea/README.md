# Galaxy Sea

The Milky Way over a sea so calm it mirrors it, and a small boat on the horizon with someone in it looking up. Stars
twinkle, slow swells ripple the reflection, and now and then a meteor crosses. After "Floating in Space", one of
Wallpaper Engine's most subscribed wallpapers.

```lua
locky.setup({ theme = "galaxysea" })
```

## What it shows

- The time and date in Didot, tonight's moon, the weather, and the battery.
- The `galaxysea` shader draws the scene. Its colors are the sky's top and horizon, then the galaxy's violet, pink
  core, and blue edge. Parameters: the horizon in points from the top (610), and the boat's x from the center (330;
  9999 hides it).
