# Memento

Memento mori for the year, after the "year progress" counters power users keep on their desktops: the day of the year,
a dot for every day of it, and a stoic's word.

```lua
locky.setup({ theme = "memento" })
```

## What it shows

- The date, and `memento.day`: Day 286 of 365, how much of the year is behind you, and the days that remain.
- `progress` as dots: one for each day of the year, the past ones filled and today in gold. Under it, how much of the
  day, week, and month is gone.
- A quote from Seneca, Marcus Aurelius, or Epictetus, changing every hour.
- The time, small, in the corner.
