# Rainy

A lo-fi window on a rainy night. Drops slide down the glass and bend the city lights behind it, which are out of
focus. The time is in words, the way you would say it.

```lua
locky.setup({ theme = "rainy" })
```

## What it shows

- The time in words ("it's ten past four"), the date, the weather, and a quote.
- The time in digits, small, in the top-right corner.
- The `rainy` shader draws the window. Its colors are the sky, the street, and warm and cool lights. Parameter 1 sets
  how much rain falls (default 1).
