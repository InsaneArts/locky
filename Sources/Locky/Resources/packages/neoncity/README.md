# Neon City

A city at 2 AM in the rain, seen from high up. Three rows of towers drift slowly past, the far ones lost in haze.
Windows glow and now and then go dark, neon signs buzz, red lights blink on the roofs, flying cars cross, and rain
streaks over all of it. The time is a neon sign.

```lua
locky.setup({ theme = "neoncity" })
```

## What it shows

- The time in pink neon and the date in cyan; the weather, the battery, and herdr's agents in the corners.
- The `neoncity` shader draws the city. Its colors are the sky, the haze, warm windows, the two neons, and the towers.
  Parameters: how fast the city drifts (1), and how much rain (1).
