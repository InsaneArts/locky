# Snowfall

A winter night in the woods. Snow falls at every depth, the near flakes large and soft. Rows of snowy pines fade into
the blue, and a cabin's windows glow warm on the snow while smoke curls from its chimney. After the snowy forest
wallpapers on Wallpaper Engine's charts.

```lua
locky.setup({ theme = "snowfall" })
```

## What it shows

- The time, the date, the days until New Year, the weather, and the battery.
- The `snowfall` shader draws the scene. Its colors are the sky's top and its low part, the snow, the pines, and the
  cabin's light. Parameters: how much snow (1), and the wind (1).
