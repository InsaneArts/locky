# Deep Blue

Under the sea, looking up. Light falls in shifting rays from a rippling surface, caustics play near the top, plankton
drifts up through the water, and every couple of minutes a whale glides slowly across, far off. After "Wake up" and the
underwater wallpapers on Wallpaper Engine's top charts.

```lua
locky.setup({ theme = "deepblue" })
```

## What it shows

- A thin clock and the date, the weather, and the battery.
- The `deepblue` shader draws the water. Its colors are the deep, the water higher up, the light at the surface, and
  the whale. Parameters: seconds between the whale's passes (150), and how bright the rays are (1).
